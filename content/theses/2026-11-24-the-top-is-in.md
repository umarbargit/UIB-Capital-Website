earlier this year, when everything was going vertical, i turned bearish.

not because ai isn't real. because markets don't trade technology. they trade liquidity. and the liquidity was running out.

i said the top was in. not wrong. just early.

### 1. money and mood

prices aren't set by what's happening. they're set by how much money is chasing them and how people feel about it.

liquidity rising: every headline is good news.<br>
liquidity draining: good news gets sold.

when great ai earnings get dumped, that's the tell.

### 2. the ipo drain

earlier this year it was obvious spacex and anthropic were both going public. it was also obvious spacex would go first.

first to ipo gets the liquidity. everyone after gets rekt.

which is exactly what happened.

spacex listed in june. $75bn+ raised at around $1.77tn.<br>
anthropic followed on 19 november. about $81bn raised, plus another $26bn privately, at around $1.5tn.

openai is too late to the party. it won't even ipo. it'll keep teasing one and never actually do it.

every dollar that goes into a new listing comes out of something else. two of the biggest raises in history, five months apart. that's a lot of money pulled out of everything else.

and don't get me started on the index rules.

nasdaq rewrote its rules so a mega ipo can get into the nasdaq-100 after 15 trading days instead of three months, and dropped the minimum float requirement. ftse russell cut it to five trading days.

so: tiny float, huge valuation, and every pension and etf holder becomes an automatic buyer within weeks, whether they wanted it or not.

guaranteed exit liquidity for insiders, paid for by your pension. they took crypto launch mechanics and brought them to the big leagues.

crime.

### 3. the midterms

i expected the midterms to go badly for the republicans. they got wiped. deservedly.

two of the worst years in american history. tariffs. insider trading on literally every announcement. stupid, pointless wars. ice.

thankfully it's coming to an end.

luckily not all voters are braindead.

for the record, i back the democrats. and lowkey, i'm not a fan of the data centre build-out or where ai is heading either. if it slows down, i won't be crying.

a democrat congress has a lot less appetite for building data centres at any cost. permits, power, subsidies. all harder from here.

### 4. what breaks

the ai build-out got priced like data centres can grow forever.

they can't. power, land, financing and angry locals are real limits.

when the money stops, half-built capacity and warehouses full of gpus end up for sale. compute gets cheap again.

ai keeps getting better. the stocks priced for the infinite version of the story don't.

the most ironic outcome: when the cycle ends, xai is the lab still standing. simply because it turned into a business that sells compute and actually makes cash.

then elon picks up the talent and mindshare from anthropic and openai, while he's already focused on satellites. democrats slow data centres on the ground. the guy already in orbit wins.

simulation things.

### 5. why crypto

i'm not saying crypto can't fall further. i'm saying it's the better horse for 2027.

stocks are still priced for the ai story. crypto already had its washout. the majors are a long way off their all-time highs.

2027 is the year it finally decouples from equities and steps back into the limelight. eth leading the way.

when the printer comes back on, crypto moves first. nothing tracks liquidity more directly.

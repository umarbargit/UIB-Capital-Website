unity is trading around $46 right now.

it seems likely that silver lake and saudi's pif will take it private at roughly $25bn. silver lake already owns about 8%. these are the same people who took ea private recently.

depending on how you count the shares, $25bn works out to roughly $52-56 a share. call it ~$54.

### 1. the business is finally working

- revenue is growing again: +32% last quarter, after shrinking through recent years
- first proper operating profit last quarter, $101m. three years ago they were losing $800m+ a year
- free cash flow was $220m in one quarter, about 35% of revenue
- most mobile games are still built on unity

fundamentals are good and getting better, which is what makes the buyout annoying. people who've held through years of it going nowhere finally get a business that works, and they'll be bought out before the market ever pays for it.

### 2. wall street still hates it

none of that seems to matter. beat earnings, sell off. ai headline, sell off. anything about applovin, godot or saas, sell off. in january one google ai demo knocked 24% off in a day.

it moves like a stock that big money is pushing around. gap up and fade, open flat and fade, gap down and keep going. it's down 76% from the 2021 high and swings almost twice as hard as the market.

### 3. the deal

i think a deal goes through. silver lake is already inside, pif has endless money, and they've done this exact trade before with ea.

but at ~$46 against a ~$54 bid, there's maybe 20% left. not worth it for me.

### 4. the trade im waiting for

1. the first bid gets rejected as too low. the stock dumps.
2. ideally this happens in the next year while the market is weak (see [the top is in](/writing/the-top-is-in)). high-beta stocks get hit hardest.
3. i buy sub $35.
4. second offer comes in ~20% higher, around $65.

that's 85%+ from $35 on a business that's already profitable and growing. solid trade.

### 5. what could go wrong

- the first bid gets accepted and i never get my price. fine, i just miss it.
- talks die and the market falls at the same time. this thing has dropped 93% before. my bear case is ~$25.

until then i'm watching.
